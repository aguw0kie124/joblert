Privacy policy

Joblert · last updated 11 October 2026

Joblert is a job application tracker. You connect a Gmail account, and Joblert reads incoming mail to log your job applications and keep their status up to date. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps, and who else sees it.

What Joblert accesses

  • Your Google account's email address and account id, to sign you in.
  • Your Gmail messages, with read-only access. Joblert cannot send, delete or change mail. It reads mail that arrives after you connect; it does not go through older mail.

What Joblert stores

  • Your email address and Google account id.
  • A Google refresh token, encrypted, so mail can be read in the background.
  • For each email judged to be about a job application: the sender, subject, date, Gmail message and thread ids, and the facts read from it (company, role, location, job id, stage, a short quote, a one-sentence summary, any deadline or interview time).
  • Applications, their status history, and anything you add or edit by hand.

Full email bodies are never stored. Mail that is not about a job application is not stored at all.

How email content is processed

To decide whether an email is about a job application and what it says, Joblert sends the sender, subject and text of the email to Groq, a model-hosting provider, which returns a classification. Obvious job alerts and marketing are dropped before this step. Email content is used only to provide the tracker to you: it is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train models by Joblert.

Who else handles the data

  • Groq, to classify emails as described above.
  • Supabase, which hosts the database.
  • Render, which hosts the application.
  • Google, which provides sign-in, Gmail access and new-mail notifications.

Data is not shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Joblert's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

"Delete account" in the app permanently removes your account, applications, stored email details and token, and revokes Joblert's access to your Google account. You can also remove access at any time from your Google account's security settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer email shown on Joblert's Google sign-in screen.
